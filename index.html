About Nick

Who you'd be training with

A few years ago, I had three kids under five, and I sat on the sofa one evening at twenty five stone (126kg) and suddenly hated how tired I was, trying to play with them. I wanted that to change, to be a role model for them rather than watching from the sofa.

If that sounds like you, or someone you love, I might be the right person to help. I'm currently training towards my Personal Trainer qualification, which I'll complete by Christmas, and I'm building my own training business alongside my ongoing work at Thrive Collective.

I've also lived with ME since I was seventeen, so I understand fatigue, low energy days, and having to work with your body rather than against it, on a personal level. That's not a specialist qualification, just real, lived understanding that I bring into every session.

Thrive

Thrive Collective

Thrive Collective is a small, welcoming personal training facility in Marsh Barton, Exeter, built around one simple idea: fitness should be accessible, enjoyable, and sustainable for everyone, at every age and stage of life. It's a private, judgement-free space that celebrates diversity and encourages everyone, whatever their starting point, to do better for themselves.

It was founded by three people who arrived at the same vision from different directions: me, Tom Bottoms, and Mark Flynn.

My part in the story

My own journey with Thrive started as a client, not a trainer. A Christmas gift of six PT sessions with Tom turned into a genuine transformation, and that experience is what led me to want to help build something that could offer the same to others. Since then I've gone from client to co-founder, and I'm now training towards my own Personal Trainer qualification, bringing that client's-eye view into my own coaching.

Who I'm privileged to work alongside

Thrive Collective's trainers each bring their own specialism and story, and I'm proud to be surrounded by this level of experience and care.

  • Tom Bottoms

    Co-Founder & Lead Personal Trainer

    Born and raised in Exeter, Tom has built his career around a space where nobody feels left behind. A Level 3 qualified Personal Trainer with additional specialist qualifications in pre- and post-natal exercise, his track record includes supporting a national swimming champion to a full athletic scholarship at Stanford and working with competitive motor sports athletes, alongside the quieter wins, like helping a new mum regain her strength, or myself with my own weight loss journey.

  • Mark Flynn

    Co-Founder & Personal Trainer

    Having gone through his own significant weight loss journey before qualifying as a PT, Mark knows first-hand what it feels like to want change and to discover what you're truly capable of. He runs dedicated men's and women's groups bringing movement and mental wellbeing together, and is currently training to become a counsellor.

  • Sarah Patterson

    Personal Trainer, Evolve Fitness Exeter

    Sarah understands what it feels like to rediscover your own body, more than once, from returning to movement after two caesareans to competing nationally in bodybuilding. A Level 3 Personal Trainer and Seated Exercise Instructor, she's currently working towards a specialist qualification in perimenopause and menopause fitness coaching.

  • Charlie Reynolds

    Personal Trainer & Founder of Cloud9 Wellness

    With over ten years of coaching experience, a degree in Physical and Outdoor Education, and Level 3 Personal Training and GP Referral qualifications, Charlie brings a calm, strategic approach to long term behaviour change through his three-phase process: Lifestyle, Build and Thrive.
